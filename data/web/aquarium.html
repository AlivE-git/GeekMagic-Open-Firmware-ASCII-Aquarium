Species, color, size, speed and bob are rolled when a fish enters the screen and stay with it until it leaves. The body undulates on its own and both the bend and the vertical bob keep time with the distance swum, so a fast fish bends and rocks faster and both turn around together with the fish. From time to time a fish picks a new speed or turns around; with Animated turns on it flips on the spot, with it off it simply snaps the other way.

Feeding

Bites With Fish can eat seaweed on, a fish swimming right over the tip of the tallest seaweed may stop and bite one bracket off, at the chance set above; while that plant grows back there is nothing else to eat. Switching it off leaves every plant at the length set in Seaweed and rules out further bites.
